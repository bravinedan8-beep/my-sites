Quick weather facts

  • Weather describes short-term conditions like rain, sunshine, wind, and humidity.
  • Climate describes the average weather of a region over decades.
  • Temperature, pressure, and moisture are key variables in the atmosphere.

Key terms

  1. Humidity: the amount of moisture in the air.
  2. Atmosphere: the layer of gases surrounding Earth.
  3. Precipitation: rain, snow, sleet, or hail.
